Quick Answers for Importers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything international buyers need to know regarding stock availability, variants, wholesale pricing, container loading, and ocean shipping.

🔍
1. How do I know if an item is ready stock or made-to-order? ▼
Every product in our catalog features a live status badge:

• Ready to Ship: In-stock inventory in our Cirebon warehouse. These can be inspected, wrapped, and moved to Tanjung Priok Port (Jakarta) within 3 to 5 business days.
• Made-to-Order: Production lead time is typically 3 to 4 weeks for full container loads (FCL), depending on the complexity of the weave and total quantities.
2. What variants, weave patterns, and finishes can I choose from? ▼
We offer extensive variant flexibility:

• Weave Patterns: Classic 1/2" French Cannage, Open Webbing, Dense Herringbone, Kubu Mud Weave, Sunburst Peacock, and Cross-Lattice.
• Finishes: Natural Honey Clear Coat, Bleached Scandinavian Blonde, Vintage Tan, Kubu Grey, Warm Walnut, and Matte Ebonized Black. All finishes utilize non-toxic, eco-friendly water-based sealers.
• Custom Dimensions: We can adjust seat heights (e.g. counter vs bar height) or overall dimensions for commercial hospitality projects.
3. How is the total price calculated, and are there volume discounts? ▼
Our standard prices are quoted in USD FOB Tanjung Priok Port (Jakarta). We also provide direct CIF quotes to your destination port upon request.

• Tier 1 (Sample / LCL): Standard wholesale price at product MOQ.
• Tier 2 (20ft Container ~28 CBM): 5% volume discount applied across order.
• Tier 3 (40ft High Cube ~68 CBM): 10% to 15% discount plus optimized nesting arrangement.
You can use our interactive RFQ tool on this website to build an instant quote with total estimated price and CBM volume!
4. How much does international shipping cost, and how long does it take to arrive? ▼
Ocean freight transit times from Tanjung Priok Port (Jakarta) to major regions:

• Singapore / SE Asia: 3 – 5 Days (~$750 - $1,050 for 40ft HC)
• Australia (Sydney/Melbourne): 12 – 16 Days (~$1,800 - $2,300 for 40ft HC)
• United States (West Coast - LA/Long Beach): 22 – 26 Days (~$2,850 - $3,450 for 40ft HC)
• United States (East Coast - NY/NJ): 30 – 36 Days (~$3,600 - $4,300 for 40ft HC)
• Western Europe (Rotterdam/Hamburg): 26 – 32 Days (~$2,600 - $3,200 for 40ft HC)
Use the Transit Estimator widget above for live estimates to your specific port.
5. What are your accepted payment terms for international orders? ▼
Standard B2B export payment terms:

• Telegraphic Transfer (T/T): 30% deposit upon proforma invoice confirmation; remaining 70% balance payable upon submission of copy of original Bill of Lading (B/L) and export shipping documents.
• Letter of Credit (L/C): Irrevocable L/C at sight accepted for orders exceeding $30,000 USD.
• Sample Orders: 100% advance via T/T or Wise for fast dispatch.
6. What export certifications and customs documents do you provide? ▼
We provide all necessary documentation for seamless customs clearance at your port:

• Official Ocean Bill of Lading (B/L)
• Commercial Invoice & Detailed Packing List with carton CBM
• Certificate of Origin (Form A, Form D, Form E, or Form AK for tariff reduction)
• Indonesian SVLK / V-Legal Timber & Rattan Legality Certificate
• Phytosanitary Certificate issued by the Indonesian Agricultural Quarantine Agency
• Official Methyl Bromide Fumigation Certificate (stamped by licensed fumigator)
