Frequently Asked Questions

Comprehensive answers to repetitive client sourcing inquiries.

🔍
1. How do I know if an item is ready stock or made-to-order? ▼
Look for the green "Ready to Ship" badge on our items. Ready-stock products depart our Cirebon workshop within 3 to 5 business days. Made-to-order collections require 3 to 4 weeks production for full container loads (FCL).
2. What variants, weaves, and finishes can I order? ▼
We offer 15+ weave techniques (Cannage, Herringbone, Kubu, Peacock) and 8 natural eco-friendly finishes (Natural Honey, Kubu Grey, Nordic Blonde, Ebonized Black, Walnut). Custom dimensions and OEM designs are welcome.
3. How is the total price calculated, and are there container discounts? ▼
Quoted in USD FOB Tanjung Priok Port (Jakarta). We offer 5% discounts on 20ft containers and 10%–15% discounts on 40ft High Cube containers.
4. How much does ocean shipping cost, and how long does it take? ▼
Transit time: Singapore 3-5 days; Australia 12-16 days; USA West Coast 22-26 days; Europe 26-32 days. Use our interactive calculator on the Shipping Guide page for live estimates.
5. What are your accepted export payment terms? ▼
Standard terms: 30% deposit via T/T to begin manufacturing; 70% balance upon submission of copy of original Bill of Lading (B/L). Irrevocable L/C at sight accepted for orders exceeding $30,000 USD.
